Actual Budget is a local-first personal finance tool built around the envelope budgeting method. Your data is stored on your own device or self-hosted server — never in someone else's cloud. Sync across devices is seamless, private, and optional.

## What Actual Budget offers

Actual is built for people who want complete control over their financial data. Everything runs locally — there's no subscription, no data harvesting, and no lock-in.

  <Card title="Envelope budgeting" icon="layer-group" href="/budgeting/overview">

  <Card title="Bank sync" icon="building-columns" href="/settings/bank-sync">

## Get up and running

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your deployment method">
    Install Actual using [Docker](/install/docker), deploy to [Fly.io](/install/fly), or download the [desktop app](/install/local) for Windows, Mac, or Linux.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your first budget">
    Follow the [Starting Fresh](/getting-started/starting-fresh) guide to set up your accounts, categories, and opening balances.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Understand envelope budgeting">
    Read [How envelope budgeting works](/getting-started/envelope-budgeting) to get the most out of Actual's zero-based approach.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your bank (optional)">
    Set up [bank sync](/settings/bank-sync) to automatically pull in transactions from your financial institutions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Actual Budget is completely free and open source under the MIT license. You own your data — always. No accounts required to get started with the desktop app.
</Note>
